## Chickpea

### Ph
- **Requirement Range/Value**: 6.0 to 8.0 pH (Moderately tolerant to alkalinity; optimal pH is 6.0 to 7.5.)
- **Source**: ICARDA Chickpea Database ([Link](https://www.icarda.org))
- **Notes**: Sensitive to saline soils; acid soils limit nodulation.

## Maize

### Ph
- **Requirement Range/Value**: 5.5 to 7.5 pH (Highly sensitive to soil salinity; optimal pH is 6.0 to 7.0.)
- **Source**: USDA NRCS ([Link](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov))
- **Notes**: Slightly acidic to neutral soils preferred; poor tolerance for highly alkaline conditions.

### Temperature
- **Requirement Range/Value**: 18.0 to 35.0 C (Optimal 20-30C; growth stops below 10C and decreases rapidly above 35C.)
- **Source**: FAO AGPC ([Link](https://www.fao.org/ag/agp/agpc/doc/gallery/maize.htm))
- **Notes**: Frost-sensitive; warm temperatures accelerate tasseling.

### Rainfall
- **Requirement Range/Value**: 500.0 to 800.0 mm (Evenly distributed rainfall of 500-800 mm during growing season.)
- **Source**: ICAR - Indian Institute of Maize Research ([Link](https://iimr.icar.gov.in))
- **Notes**: Waterlogging at seedling stage reduces yield drastically.

### Soil_texture
- **Requirement Range/Value**: None to None text (Deep, fertile loams or sandy loams with excellent drainage.)
- **Source**: USDA Extension ([Link](https://extension.unl.edu))
- **Notes**: Heavy clays cause root suffocation if saturated.

## Rice

### Ph
- **Requirement Range/Value**: 5.0 to 8.0 pH (Optimal 5.5-6.5; tolerable from 5.0 to 8.0 with moderate yield impact.)
- **Source**: FAO Crop Water Information ([Link](https://www.fao.org/land-water/databases-chemical-properties/crop-information/en/))
- **Notes**: Tolerates wide range but acidic clay-loams are ideal.

### Temperature
- **Requirement Range/Value**: 20.0 to 38.0 C (Optimal range 22-32C; temperatures below 15C cause sterile spikelets.)
- **Source**: International Rice Research Institute (IRRI) ([Link](https://www.irri.org))
- **Notes**: High heat during flowering (>35C) induces spikelet sterility.

### Rainfall
- **Requirement Range/Value**: 1000.0 to 2500.0 mm (Requires continuous flooding or saturated conditions; minimum 1000 mm without active irrigation.)
- **Source**: IRRI Rice Knowledge Bank ([Link](http://www.knowledgebank.irri.org))
- **Notes**: Upland rice requires less, but lowland flooded rice requires consistent water depth.

### Soil_texture
- **Requirement Range/Value**: None to None text (Heavy clay, clay loam, or silty clay soils with low water permeability.)
- **Source**: ICAR - National Rice Research Institute ([Link](https://nrri.icar.gov.in))
- **Notes**: Sandy soils are highly unsuitable due to excessive leaching of nutrients and water.

### Irrigation
- **Requirement Range/Value**: None to None text (Commonly required (flooded basins) to maintain 5-10cm water standing unless rainfall is exceptionally high.)
- **Source**: FAO Land & Water ([Link](https://www.fao.org))
- **Notes**: Water requirement ranges from 1200 mm to 2000 mm per crop cycle depending on evaporation.
