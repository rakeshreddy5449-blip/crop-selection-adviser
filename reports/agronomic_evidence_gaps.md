## Priority gaps for follow-up research

1. Locate crop-specific primary publications/pages with clear citations and accessible text for the 21 crops with no verified parameter evidence in this pass.
2. Research rice pH, temperature, rainfall, and soil texture independently. Keep rainfall separate from irrigation/seasonal field water input and record lowland/upland contexts separately where the source does so.
3. For each numerical claim, identify whether the value is an acceptable limit, optimum, observed range, or context-dependent amount. Do not combine these meanings into one minimum/maximum pair.
4. For irrigation and rainfall, identify rainfed and irrigated systems separately, with season, region, soil, cultivar, and crop stage where reported.
5. Capture publication title, author/institution, direct page/PDF URL, pinpoint section/table/page, and the supporting passage before assigning a verified status.
